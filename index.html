About

I’m Lola, a designer based in Manchester.

I did a BTEC in Graphic Design, then a BSc in Computer Science (First-Class). Since then I’ve worked mostly in hand-painted and customised product, alongside some digital design and content work.

For the past six years I’ve done commercial work for brands including Adidas, Timberland and E4, turning briefs into finished pieces to a deadline. I’ve worked with clients and small teams throughout, and I’m interested in how people actually use what gets made.

I’m now looking for a design role on the digital side, where I can combine the practical craft with my technical background. If you think I could be a fit, please get in touch.
